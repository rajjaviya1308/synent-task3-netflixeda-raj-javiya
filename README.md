Task 3: Netflix Data Analysis - What's Actually Popular?

This time, I took a look at Netflix data since, let's be honest, who doesn't love Netflix?
The Question

Netflix has over 8,000 shows and movies. But:
- What are the most commonly available items?
- Which countries are producing the best content?
- Which genres are currently popular?
- When is new content usually added?

These were some of the things I wanted to uncover!

What I Discovered

The Numbers at a Glance

- Total Content: 8,800+ titles
- Movies: 6,100 (70%) - So, more movies than TV shows
- TV Shows: 2,700 (30%) - But we all love to binge TV shows!
- Countries Represented: 140+ (It's a global market!)

Where Does the Content Come From?

Top 5 Countries by number of titles:
1. USA - As expected, it's in the lead with 2,500+ titles
2. India - Shows a rapidly growing content library with 1,000+ titles
3. UK - A strong contender with 500+ titles
4. Canada - Our North American neighbors with 400+ titles
5. France - With 300+ titles

A notable point is that India is growing at a rapid pace and might soon overtake some other countries.

What Genres are Trending?

Most Popular Genres:
1. Drama - Always a crowd-pleaser
2. Comedy - Who doesn't need a good laugh?
3. Documentary - People are interested in learning new things
4. Action - Sometimes we just need some explosions!
5. International - Diversity in content is appreciated

The Rating System

Most Common Rating: TV-MA (Mature Audience)

This indicates Netflix's focus on providing content for adult audiences.
Other ratings are present but TV-MA is by far the most common.

When Does New Content Arrive?

An Interesting Pattern:
- November and December are peak months for content addition.
- This suggests a holiday strategy to cater to people's free time during this period.
- January, on the other hand, is the slowest month (perhaps after people have had their fill).

Best Content Through the Years

- Oldest content: From 1925 (a classic!)
- Newest content: 2021
- Peak year for content addition: 2019
- Trend: More recent content is being added at a faster rate.

- The USA remains the top content producer.

What I Learned

1.  Netflix is Truly Global: Content is sourced from all over the world.
2.  Proven Genres are Key: Drama and Comedy always deliver, and Netflix continues to invest in them.
3.  Timing is Crucial: The holiday season is a clear indicator of a strategy to maximize viewership when people are looking for entertainment.
4.  USA's Dominance: While other countries are catching up, the USA still produces the most content.
5.  Focus on Adults: The high number of TV-MA rated content highlights a clear target audience.

What This Means for Netflix

-   Strategy: Continue focusing on adult content, reliable genres (drama/comedy), and global markets.
-   Growth Areas: India is showing significant potential and should be closely monitored.
-   Timing: Utilize the holiday season for strategic content releases.
-   Global Reach: Maintain a diverse library of content from many different countries to cater to a broad audience.
-   Risk Aversion: Stick with proven, successful genres to minimize investment risk.
